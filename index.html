Kori Jazz Portfolio

At Kori Jazz we play street basketball and listen to jazz. Come listen to the music and enjoy the atmosphere, or jump on the court to join the games! We organize one or two bigger events a year, plus a handful of pop-up events with a single band. All events are free of charge and open to everyone!
