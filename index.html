Kori Jazz Portfolio

Live jazz and open street basketball, on the same court. Events from 2022 to 2026.

Concept

Kori Jazz began with a simple idea: bring live jazz and open street basketball into the same public space. The name "The Bridge" nods to Sonny Rollins' 1962 album — Rollins famously practiced on the Williamsburg Bridge in New York because he had nowhere else to play. In 2023 we found our own bridge, by accident: the underpass of the Hämeentie bridge between Vallila and Hermanni, Helsinki, taken as shelter from the rain. The atmosphere — graffiti, acoustics, traffic passing overhead — turned out to be exactly right. The bridge is now scheduled for demolition, which gives the footage from earlier years added documentary weight.
