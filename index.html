Kori Jazz Portfolio

Kori Jazz is a Helsinki-based event concept that brings live jazz and open street basketball together in public urban space.

Since its first event in 2022, it has grown from a single gathering into a recurring program: one or two larger events each year, complemented by a handful of pop-ups in various settings. In 2025 the concept expanded internationally with an event in Copenhagen.

Every event is free of charge and open to everyone, with no sign-up or team required.

2026

Kori Jazz The Bridge 2026

Aug 22, 2026 · Hermanni Basketball Court, Helsinki

The 2026 main event drew a record crowd despite unsettled weather. The lineup included Kadi Vija, winner of the 2026 Jazz Emma award. The day also featured an open jam session, dunk and jelly contests, and a dedicated court time for women and non-binary players. Attendees could also get the Kori Jazz logo silkscreened onto their own shirts.

2025

Kori Jazz The Bridge 2025

Aug 16, 2025 · Hermanni Basketball Court, Helsinki

Kori Jazz's last event of the summer returned to Hermanni Basketball Court under the Hämeentie bridge. Around 300 people came despite light rain. Beyond the regular lineup, a surprise saxophone duo, Vili Järvinen and Antti Vauhkonen, played between sets, and the night closed with an open jam session. The podcast "Kasper ja Mikko – Suomen suosituin podcast" also stopped by to record an episode.

Lineup

Arttu Huopainen South-East Corner · Viive · Myrtsi

Poster: Veera Kesänen, Photos: Atte Männikkö & Valtteri Moilanen

Kori Jazz Giant Steps

Jul 11, 2025 · Urban 13, Copenhagen

Kori Jazz's first event abroad took place at Urban 13 in Copenhagen, part of Copenhagen Jazz Week. Denmark's Køs and the international act Children Of Fish played alongside Finland's Solaris Quartet. Bornebasketfonden and Dime ran the basketball side of the day, opening with games for kids before handing the court over to everyone else. Despite rain, around 200 people showed up, as did Denmark's biggest jazz radio station P8, there to cover the event and interview Children Of Fish.

Lineup

Køs (DK) · Children Of Fish (INT) · Solaris Quartet (FIN)

2023

Kori Jazz 2023

Aug 19, 2023 · Hermanni Basketball Court, Helsinki

Kori Jazz 2023 was originally planned for Hietsu, but rain pushed it under the Hämeentie bridge at Hermanni Basketball Court instead. The spot turned out to be exactly right, and Kori Jazz has returned there every year since. The lineup also included the first international booking, Estonia's TamTam.

Lineup

Antje Rößeler Duo (DEU/FIN) · TamTam (EST) · Nuoku

2022

Kori Jazz 2022

Aug 10, 2022 · Hietsu Basketball Court, Helsinki

The first Kori Jazz took place at Hietsu Basketball Court. Alongside open pick-up games, the event featured a 3x3 tournament with advance sign-up. The basketball side of the day was organized by Yökoris, who have run the courts at almost every Kori Jazz event since.
